## Understanding Island Settings in Fortnite Creative

**כותרת מקורית:** Understanding Island Settings in Fortnite Creative  
**כתובת מקור:** https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/understanding-island-settings-in-fortnite-creative

**
Island Settings** are the settings you can control that affect the island you're developing. These settings are available when you're in [Create mode](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#create-mode) and are inaccessible [in-game](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#ingame), with a few exceptions.

Island Settings is one of the tabs available on the **Creative menu** nav bar. From Create mode:

-
Use the **M** key to open the **Creative Menu **then select the **Island Settings** tab.

Use the **B** key to open the **Quick Menu**

then select the Island Settings tab.

You can also press either key to **toggle back** to your island.

In addition to the toggle keys above, you can:

-
Press the **Esc** key, then select **Island Settings** from the options on the right side of the screen.

### Island Settings vs. Game Settings

**Island Settings** define the rules and structure for a [game mode](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#game-mode), and rounds, environments, and settings that apply to all players and objects during a game for a single island.

**Game settings** (found under the [Game Menu](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-sidebar-and-game-menu-in-fortnite-creative)) are a player's **personal settings** that affect the way that player interacts with Fortnite games as a whole, from defining graphics settings to how the player inputs information.

The primary difference between Island Settings and Game Settings is that **game developers use Island Settings** to control how players interact with the island and to establish the game rules, while **players use the Game Settings** to customize their personal experience across all islands.

### The Island Settings UI

Island Settings establish a baseline of behavior on your island, but you can override many of these settings when customizing various devices.

To customize settings for your island, make sure you're on the right tab, then select your category and subcategory.

-
Select **Island Settings** from the menu tabs at the top of the screen.

-
Select a **category**.

-
Click a **subcategory** to show the available settings.

If you're looking for a specific setting, you can use the **search bar** to enter some or all of the setting name.

Categories with matches to the search term are indicated as shown above.

Across the bottom of the screen for all island settings is a row of buttons.

 Button Description
**Restore Defaults**

Click this to reset all edits you've made in the island settings back to their default values. Be aware that doing this will lose any changes you've made to any of the options.

**Start Game (Hold)**

Clicking and holding this button accomplishes the same thing as clicking **Start Game** on the game menu.

**Event Browser**

Opens the **Event Browser** — covered on the [Event Browser](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/event-browser-in-fortnite-creative)** **page.

**Exit**

Return to the island.

If you restore defaults, be aware that if the defaults for island settings have changed since the island was created, restoring defaults will update the values to the new default values, which could result in some unexpected behavior.

For example, if an island were created before the [Mantle](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) feature was added, and the default for new islands is now to have Mantle on, restoring defaults will turn on Mantle.

## Edit Mode Settings in Fortnite Creative

**כותרת מקורית:** Edit Mode Settings in Fortnite Creative  
**כתובת מקור:** https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/edit-mode-settings-in-fortnite-creative  
**מקור קלט:** `03_הגדרות_אי_וחוקי_משחק(1).md`  
**היקף:** Creative / Shared בהתאם לעמוד המקור  
**מזהה עמוד:** `edit-mode-settings-in-fortnite-creative`

*

The [Creative toolset](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#creative-toolset) has modes, or ways of doing things. [**Edit mode**](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/uefn/unreal-editor-for-fortnite-glossary#edit-mode) is a term used primarily in **Unreal Editor for Fortnite** but is similar to what's called [Create mode](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#create-mode) in **Fortnite Creative**. What these modes have in common is that they are modes in which you can edit what's on your island as opposed to [Play mode](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#play-mode). (Edit Mode is used here because these settings are also shared with UEFN).

This is the mode you're in when you access your island from the [hub](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#hub). In this mode, you can access [inventory](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#inventory) items and island settings, you can place [props](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#prop) and [devices](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#device) on the island, and you can customize the devices you place.

The **Edit Mode** category provides ways to control settings so they better suit your needs when creating your island. From the **Island Settings** tab, click **Edit Mode**, then click a **subcategory** to expand it.

Any changes you make to these settings are automatically saved. You can restore the settings to their original values at any time by clicking the **Restore Defaults** button. This will reset only the settings for your current category.

Following are the Edit Mode settings. You can toggle these settings **On** or **Off**.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### HUD - Show Health and Shield

- **Value / Values:** Defaults to On.
- **Description:** Shows health and shield while you're in Edit/Create mode unless toggled off.

##### HUD - Show UI Under Minimap

- **Value / Values:** Defaults to On.
- **Description:** Shows the minimap while you're in Edit/Create mode unless toggled off.

##### Show Resources

- **Value / Values:** Defaults to On.
- **Description:** Shows resources while you're in Edit/Create mode unless toggled off.

##### Enable Pickaxe

- **Value / Values:** Defaults to On.
- **Description:** If you toggle this off, your avatar will not have a pickaxe during Edit/Create mode. You will still have to activate the phone tool.

##### Enable Vehicle Damage

- **Value / Values:** Defaults to Off.
- **Description:** Determines whether vehicles can inflict damage in Edit/Create mode.

## Mode Settings in Fortnite Creative

**כותרת מקורית:** Mode Settings in Fortnite Creative  
**כתובת מקור:** https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/mode-settings-in-fortnite-creative  
**מקור קלט:** `03_הגדרות_אי_וחוקי_משחק(1).md`  
**היקף:** Creative / Shared בהתאם לעמוד המקור  
**מזהה עמוד:** `mode-settings-in-fortnite-creative`

A **mode** is how you see or experience something. In **Fortnite**, a [game mode](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#game-mode) is about who can play, and how.

The Mode settings are where you can set things like when and how your game starts, where and when players can spawn into the game, how to assign and track scores, and other factors that influence or control gameplay.

Under Mode, there are a number of subcategories you can modify to change gameplay, and many of these subcategories have multiple settings. From the **Island Settings tab**, click the **Mode category**, then click a **subcategory** to expand it.

If you know the name of a setting you want to change, use the **search bar** to find it.

Island settings provide a baseline for your game, but some device settings, such as Team Settings & Inventory and [Class Designer](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-class-designer-devices-in-fortnite-creative), can override these settings.

Any changes you make are automatically saved. You can restore the settings to their original values at any time by clicking the **Restore Defaults** button. This will reset only the settings for your current selected category.

The following sections describe the settings available in each subcategory, and how you can use them.

Some settings are grayed out. This usually indicates that another setting must be changed before that setting is available.

### Structure

**Structure **addresses the basic structure of your game: player limits, team member distribution, and numbers of rounds.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Max Players

- **Value / Values:** 16, 1–100
- **Description:** Set the maximum number of players that can play at the same time.

##### Teams

- **Value / Values:** Free for All, Cooperative, Custom, Pick a number 2–100
- **Description:** Set the number of teams players will be divided into. Custom lets players pick their own teams using the Switch Team menu option. Note that if you set Teams to Custom and leave Team Size at Dynamic, the matchmaking feature will not work.

##### Team Size

- **Value / Values:** Dynamic, Split Evenly, Pick a number 1–4
- **Description:** Dynamic allows asymmetric team sizes.

##### Total Rounds

- **Value / Values:** 1, Pick a number up to 100
- **Description:** This sets the total number of rounds in a game.

### Matchmaking Settings

[Matchmaking](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/fortnite-creative-glossary) is how players match up in video games.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Island Matchmaking Privacy

- **Value / Values:** Party Choice, Public, Private
- **Description:** Public games can be joined by anyone. Private games are restricted to groups of players in the same party. Party Choice gives the player the option of choosing either public or private.

##### Team Fill Option

- **Value / Values:** Must Fill, Party Choice
- **Description:** You can only access this option when Team Size under Structure is set between 2 and 4. If set to Party Choice, the players can choose their teams. If set to Must Fill, players are automatically assigned to teams.

##### Minimum Players

- **Value / Values:** 8, Pick a number
- **Description:** The minimum number of players required to start a game session.At the end of the overtime phase, the queue will be canceled if it has not reached the number of players indicated here.

##### Overtime Player Target

- **Value / Values:** 8, Pick a number
- **Description:** The target number of players that triggers the queue to send players into a match during the queue's overtime phase.

##### Queue Main Duration

- **Value / Values:** 5 s, Pick a time
- **Description:** Determines how long the Main Phase for matchmaking lasts. During the queue's main phase, the queue attempts to achieve a full match.

##### Queue Overtime Duration

- **Value / Values:** 5 s, Pick a time
- **Description:** Determines how long the Queue Overtime Phase for matchmaking lasts. During the queue's overtime phase, the queue attempts to reach the Overtime Player Target.

##### Matchmaking Max Team Count

- **Value / Values:** 4, Pick a number
- **Description:** The number of teams the session expects to have.

##### Matchmaking Max Players Per Session

- **Value / Values:** 32, Pick a number
- **Description:** Maximum number of players matchmaking allows to join a session.

### Team Settings

**Team settings** deal primarily with how team members identify each other and distinguish themselves from other teams.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Allow Friendly Fire

- **Value / Values:** No, Yes
- **Description:** Set whether players on the same team can take damage from each other.

##### Team Rotation

- **Value / Values:** Disabled, Select a number of rounds
- **Description:** Set how often teams should be rotated. Rotation is not supported if teams are asymmetric, or if Teams is set to Cooperative under Structure.

##### UI Team Colors

- **Value / Values:** Relationship, Team Color
- **Description:** With the default Relationship, the friendly team uses blue as the team color, and the opposing team red. When set to Team Color, each team is represented by its specified color.

##### Team Visuals Determined At

- **Value / Values:** Round Start, Game Start
- **Description:** Determines whether team names and colors stay as assigned at game start, or change at start of each round.

### Class Settings

**Class **refers to any set of [attributes](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/fortnite-creative-glossary#attribute) or [inventories](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/fortnite-creative-glossary#inventory) (weapons, items, and so on) that can be assigned to a player or group of players. The values you assign here are a baseline for your game, but can be overridden by some devices.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Default Class Identifier

- **Value / Values:** No Class, Pick a class number
- **Description:** Determine the default class for players at game start, or in the event that their class is reset by a device.

##### Revert to Default Class At

- **Value / Values:** End of Game, End of Round, Player Eliminated
- **Description:** Determines when a player's class should be reset.

### Game Start Settings

**Game start ****settings **control the elements that affect when your game starts.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Auto Start

- **Value / Values:** Off, Immediate, Pick a time
- **Description:** Auto Start settings other than Off only work with published islands. When set to Off, the game will start when manually activated on a private game, and start with a 90-second countdown on public games. For a published island, you can set this to Immediate, or select a time.

##### Game Start Countdown

- **Value / Values:** 3 Seconds, Off, Pick a time
- **Description:** Set the countdown time once a game starts. This setting applies to both game and round start.

##### Force Start at Max Players

- **Value / Values:** Off, On
- **Description:** Game will start once the maximum number of players is reached.

##### Force Start Delay

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a time
- **Description:** Puts a time delay on a forced start.

### Spawning Settings

**Spawning settings** define where and how players spawn or respawn in a game.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Spawn Location

- **Value / Values:** Spawn Pads, Sky, Current Location, Do Not Spawn
- **Description:** Set where players will spawn at game start.

##### Spawn Pad Selection

- **Value / Values:** Random, Near Teammates
- **Description:** At the start of a game, the selected spawn pad will always be random, but respawns have the option of remaining random or spawning players near their teammates.

##### Respawn Type

- **Value / Values:** Individual, Wave
- **Description:** If you change this setting to Wave, eliminated players will respawn together. Otherwise, eliminated players will spawn one at a time.

##### Respawn Time

- **Value / Values:** 5 Seconds, Pick a time
- **Description:** The amount of time a player must wait before they can spawn back into the game.

##### Spawn Immunity Time

- **Value / Values:** 5 Seconds, Pick a time
- **Description:** Determines how long a player is invulnerable after spawning. This can be overridden if you are using a Team Settings & Inventory device or Class Designer device. Note that this setting is only active if Override Spawn Immunity Time is set to Yes.

##### Override Spawn Immunity Time

- **Value / Values:** No, Yes
- **Description:** Determines if the default invulnerability time granted to a player on respawn should be overridden.

##### Only Allow Respawn if Spawn Pads Found

- **Value / Values:** Off, On
- **Description:** If this is set to On, a player can only respawn to a spawn pad after elimination, and never from the sky.

##### Spawn Limit

- **Value / Values:** Infinite, Pick a number
- **Description:** This sets how many times a player can spawn in the game. If any amount other than Infinite, the initial spawn counts as 1, so letting a player respawn twice after start of game would require a limit of 3.

##### Join in Progress

- **Value / Values:** Spawn on Next Round, Spawn, Spectate, Pick a team number
- **Description:** Determines how a player joining mid-game will be treated. If you set this to Spawn, a player can join in at any point in the game.

##### Post Game Spawn Location

- **Value / Values:** Island Start, Pre-Game Location
- **Description:** With the default Island Start setting, players will respawn on the Island Start spawn pads, or in the sky. If set to Pre-Game Location, players will return to their start-of-game positions.

##### After Last Spawn Go To

- **Value / Values:** Spectating, Pick a team number
- **Description:** Determines where a player goes when all their spawns are used up.

### Eliminations Settings

**Elimination settings** help to define consequences when a player is eliminated.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Down But Not Out

- **Value / Values:** Default, Classic, Off, Improved
- **Description:** Determines the DBNO state. Default sets automatically based on team size: for a team of 1 player, this will be Off. For a team of 2 or more, Classic is applied as a default. Improved adds interactions like opening doors and dropping inventory items. Off disables this setting entirely.

##### Drop Reboot Card on Elimination

- **Value / Values:** Off, On, Don't Override
- **Description:** If set to On, a player will drop a Reboot Card when eliminated. This only works if a remaining teammate is able to use a Reboot Van.

##### Squad Multi Interaction

- **Value / Values:** On, Off
- **Description:** If set to On, any teammate can interact with a DBNO player. If Off, only one player (the first one to the downed player's aid) can interact.

##### Eliminated Player's Items

- **Value / Values:** Drop, Keep, Delete
- **Description:** Determines what happens to a player's items when eliminated. If set to Drop, anyone can pick up the dropped items. If Keep, the player will respawn with those items. If Delete, the items are removed from play.

##### Health Granted on Elimination

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a value
- **Description:** The amount of health granted when a player eliminates another player. Any health above the player's max health is awarded as shields.

##### Wood Granted on Elimination

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a value
- **Description:** The amount of wood granted when a player eliminates another player.

##### Stone Granted on Elimination

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a value
- **Description:** The amount of stone granted when a player eliminates another player.

##### Metal Granted on Elimination

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a value
- **Description:** The amount of metal granted when a player eliminates another player.

##### Gold Granted on Elimination

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a value
- **Description:** The amount of gold granted when a player eliminates another player.

##### Allow Manual Respawning

- **Value / Values:** Yes, No
- **Description:** Determines whether the player can use the respawn menu while the game is in progress.

##### Player Elimination Audio

- **Value / Values:** On, Off
- **Description:** If On, audio effects will play when a player is eliminated.

### Scoring Settings

**Scoring settings** define ways that you can award players a score.

**אפשרויות, ערכים ותיאורים:**

##### Vehicle Trick Score Multiplier

- **Value / Values:** 1.0, Pick a number
- **Description:** Use this setting to apply a multiplier to any trick score earned with a vehicle.

##### Show Individual Scores

- **Value / Values:** No, Yes
- **Description:** If set to No, individual player scores will display on the scoreboard. If set to Yes, the cumulative score for all team members will display.

##### Time Alive Start Point

- **Value / Values:** Player Spawn, Round Start
- **Description:** Determines when a player's time alive starts a count.

##### Time Alive Team Tracking Method

- **Value / Values:** Sum, Longest, Shortest
- **Description:** If counting time alive for a team, it can be based on the Sum of all players' time alive, on the player on the team with the Longest time alive, or the player with the Shortest time alive.

##### Use Team Score

- **Value / Values:** No, Yes
- **Description:** If set to Yes, teams are scored by the sum of all player scores for that team. This allows teams to retain all score if a player leaves the team or the game.

##### Elimination Score

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a number
- **Description:** The amount of score awarded when a player eliminates another player.

##### Assist Score

- **Value / Values:** 0, Pick a number
- **Description:** The amount of score awarded to a player when they help to eliminate another player but don't take the final elimination.

### Voice Chat Settings

**Voice chat settings** control how players can communicate with each other in-game.

 Option Values Description
Voice Chat Scope

Team, All, None

This defines the behavior of the Game Channel. Island Settings never affect the Party Channel. Individual players' settings and permissions may further restrict with whom a player can communicate.

**All**: Chat on the Game Channel is unrestricted.

**Team**: Players see only their team members on the Game Channel.

**None**: Disables the Game Channel for all players.

Attenuate Voice

**
In **Island Settings**, the **Overview** category provides access to tools that you can use to describe and publish an island. You can also make backups and restore from those backups if needed, and reset the island to its original settings.

Click the **Overview** category, then click an option in the Tools **subcategory to access the settings for that tool.

### Edit Island Name and Description

This option opens a window where you can edit the island name and description.

Your island name can be up to 40 characters.

Each line of the island description can take up to 150 characters, for a total of 450. This is a perfect place to start [onboarding players](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/onboarding-players-in-fortnite-creative).

When you change the name here, the new name appears in your console on the hub next time you access Creative. It also shows up in [Discover](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) when you look at an island details screen.

*1. is the island name. 2. is the island description.*

Your island name and description shows up in the [Creator Portal](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#creator-portal), and any changes you make in Creator Portal will also show up here. (For more info, see [Publishing from the Creator Portal](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/publishing-from-the-creator-portal-in-fortnite-creative)).

### Publish Island

Click the [Creator Portal](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/creator-portal-overview-in-fortnite-creative) link to log in to the Creator Portal if you're ready to publish your island. Click **Copy Private Code** to get a link you can share for playtesting.

Note that you can only publish your island if you're enrolled in the Creator Portal program, but you can still share your island with friends if you're not.

### Create Island Backup

Selecting this option immediately starts creating a backup of your island, and returns you to the island in Create mode.

This is useful if you want to make some major changes but you're not sure if they'll work and want to be able to return to a previous version if they don't.

If you return to the Overview category while the server is backing up your island, you'll see that both this option and the **Reset Island to Original State** option are grayed out. There's also a message saying "On cooldown" while the island is saved or reset.

### Restore from Backup

Selecting this option will immediately start to restore the most recent backup of your island.

Click **Confirm** to complete the backup. You'll be returned to the island in Create mode while the island is restored. The length of time for the restore will vary based on how much memory you've used.

### Reset Island to Original State

This option will restore your island to its original state, meaning that you'll have the island in the same state as when you first created it.

This option removes all props and devices you've placed on the island, but leaves the name and description intact.

This is useful if you want to start over with a clean slate. You will be returned to the hub while the island reboots.

Games are divided into **rounds**. A round is a single play in a game to a specific end result, and a game can have only one round or many rounds. A round can be based on a time limit, an accumulation of points, or the accomplishment of some other objective.

Games can be won based on individual rounds won. Gameplay can change between rounds. This would include changes like team assignments, weapons, and so on.

The **Round category** is where you define such settings as end conditions and victory conditions for each round, and what happens when a round is complete. Each of these can also be defined for the overall game on Mode Settings. There are multiple round categories that you can modify to change gameplay, and many of these have multiple settings.

From the **Island Settings** tab, click the **Round category**, then click a **subcategory** to expand the settings.

If you know the name of a setting you want to change, use the **search box** to find it.

Any changes you make to these settings are automatically saved. You can restore the settings to their original values at any time by clicking the **Restore Defaults** button. This will reset only the settings for your current category.

The following sections describe the settings available in each subcategory, and how you can use them.

Some settings are grayed out. This usually indicates that another setting must be changed before that setting is available.

A rim light is a backlight behind a character that makes the character stand out from the background. This setting determines how strong the rim light is.

**Fog Thickness**

**Default**, Pick a value

Fog is an element that can add an eerie quality to your island. If this is set to **Default** the result is no fog. The higher the percentage you pick, the thicker the fog. Setting this to 100% setting makes a dense fog that limits visibility dramatically.

**Fog Color**

**Default**, Pick a color

As with the **Light Color** setting, applying a color to the fog can change the mood of the island.

**Don't Override**

The **pre-game lobby** is where players initially spawn as they wait to join games. Use pre-game lobbies as a dedicated space for players to learn game rules while [matchmaking](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary).

Design lobbies to communicate game rules by using informational devices like [**Billboard**](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-billboard-devices-in-fortnite-creative) and [Pop-Up Dialog](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-popup-dialog-devices-in-fortnite-creative).

### Building Your Lobby

Browse the [Prefab](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) and [Gallery](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) tabs and consider a [theme](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) for your pre-game lobby.

Use items from the **PreFab** tab if you want to use a pre-built structure, complete with props and decorations.

Use items from the **Gallery** tab if you want to build a unique pre-game lobby, putting pieces together on your own. Challenge yourself by mixing pieces from different galleries.

Build structures that are at least two tiles high, with a floor and a roof.

For a faster [build](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#build), press **B** to open the **Options** panel ([Quick Menu](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#quick-menu)). Locate and set **Building to Prop** to **Off**.

Follow the steps below to create a pre-game structure.

*Click image to enlarge.*

-
Press the **Tab** key to open the [Creative inventory](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary).

-
From the **Prefabs** or **Galleries** tab, select your floor piece, then click **PLACE NOW**.

-
Press **V** until the **Grid Snap** option is 2. By setting [grid snap](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#grid-snap) to 2, the pieces will automatically align to the island’s [grid](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#grid) for easier placement.

-
Place your floor pieces to create the pre-game lobby's foundation.

Make the pre-game lobby large enough to hold the maximum number of players for your game.

Follow these steps to select your wall piece.

-
From the **Prefabs** and **Galleries** tab, select your wall piece.

-
Click PLACE NOW, and [place](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#place) the wall pieces around the front and back edge of the floors. Build the walls at least two tiles high.

-
With the wall still equipped, press the **Tab** key until the left sidebar reads **Rotation Axis [YAW]**.

-
Align the wall pieces along the sides of the structure at least two tiles high.

The lobby should look similar to the photo below.

Select a roof piece for the lobby.

-
Press the **Tab** key to open the **Creative** menu, then click the **Prefabs** or **Galleries** tab.

-
Select your desired roof, then click PLACE NOW to place the piece.

-
Align the roof pieces around the top perimeter of the wall pieces to create a seamless roof.

The pre-game lobby’s structure is now complete.
