## Install and Launch Fortnite Creative and Unreal Editor for Fortnite

**
Ready to explore the toolsets available for Fortnite? Find out how to set up your Epic Games account and download everything you need, for free! Yep, for free!

-
Fortnite Creative** is a sandbox game where you can create and save your island experience using the Creative toolset.

-
**Unreal Editor for Fortnite (UEFN)** is a game engine system composed of a collection of editors that let you build your terrain, and add props and game mechanics with more control that you have in Fortnite, and where you can also add other features, like controlled lighting and sculpt your landscapes to make your games even more uniquely yours.

-
**Verse **is a programming language designed to work both within and outside of game design. UEFN includes an interface that you can use to customize devices even further using Verse.

Together, these apps make up Fortnite.

To use any of these tools, you will need to:

-
Create an Epic Games account if you don't already have one.

-
Download the Epic Games Launcher.

-
From the launcher, download Fortnite.

-
From the launcher, download Unreal Editor for Fortnite (UEFN). Verse downloads with UEFN.

## Exploring the Content Browser Menu in Fortnite Creative

**כותרת מקורית:** Exploring the Content Browser Menu in Fortnite Creative  
**כתובת מקור:** https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative  
**מקור קלט:** `02_ממשק_משתמש_תפריטים_ומקשים(1).md`  
**היקף:** Creative / Shared בהתאם לעמוד המקור  
**מזהה עמוד:** `exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative`

The **Content** browser is where you can access the Fortnite Creative inventory and tools to create your gameplay experiences.

Follow the steps below to access the **Content** browser.

-
Spawn into your Creative island.

-
Press **~** on your keyboard to select the **Phone Tool**.

-
Press **Tab** on your keyboard to open the **Content** browser.

The assets within the **Content** browser are updated with every update. With this growing inventory, you can easily use sidebars and filters to find assets by name, theme, and even tags.

### Content Browser Sidebars

The Creative inventory holds all of the assets to create both the environment and mechanics of your unique gameplay experiences.

Because of the wide range of tools offered, you can easily navigate through assets using the left and right sidebars.

The left sidebar displays the [Categories panel](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative#categories-panel), which contains a **search bar** and categories to help you locate the assets offered. The right sidebar displays both the [Details panel](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative#details-panel) and **Filters** tabs to sort through assets by tags.

Press **CTRL + R**, or select the side arrows to expand or collapse the left and right panels.

#### Categories Panel

The left sidebar is the **Categories** panel, which sorts the Creative inventory assets into categories. You can also use the search bar to search for assets by name.

Within the **Categories** sidebar, you can expand the contents displayed for the following categories that contain the Creative inventory:

-
[Home](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative#home)

-
[Prefabs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative)

-
[Gallery](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative)

-
[Devices](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative#devices)

-
[Weapons](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative#weapons)

-
[Items](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative#items)

-
[Chest](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-content-browser-menu-in-fortnite-creative#chest)

#### Home

Opening the **Content** browser will take you to the **Home** category. This displays a collection of featured assets from each category below it.

#### Prefabs and Galleries

Hone your level design skills by using assets from the [Prefabs and Galleries](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-prefabs-and-galleries-in-fortnite-creative) categories.

The **Prefabs** category holds sets of pre-built structures furnished with props. The **Galleries** category holds individual assets sorted by theme that contain building assets and props to build and decorate your environment.

Combine assets from these categories to create unique environments like [Lava Cave](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/uefn/lava-cave-in-unreal-editor-for-fortnite) that suit your gameplay's theme.

#### Devices

Create game mechanics on your island with the assets offered from the [Devices](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-devices-in-fortnite) category. This category contains a wide range of devices you can use to create an infinite variety of unique gameplay experiences.

Combine devices like the [Player Spawner](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-player-spawn-pad-devices-in-fortnite-creative) and [Post Process](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-post-processing-devices-in-fortnite-creative) device to create gameplays such as [**Prop Hunt**](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/uefn/verse-prop-hunt-template-in-unreal-editor-for-fortnite) that leave your players craving more.

For more inspiration on how to use devices, see [Device Design Examples](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/device-design-examples-in-fortnite-creative).

#### Weapons

Use assetss from the **Weapons** category to add the necessary weapons for your players to use during gameplay.

Combine weapons with devices like the [Item Spawner](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-item-spawner-devices-in-fortnite-creative) **Sideways Minigun** to recreate combat games such as [Creature Rush](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/design-a-creature-rush-game-in-fortnite-creative).

#### Items

The **[Items](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-items-in-fortnite-creative)** category contains assets that players can use to fulfill device requirements and add status effects.

Pair devices like the **[Conditional Button](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-conditional-button-devices-in-fortnite-creative)** with [objective items](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-objective-items-in-fortnite-creative) to create gameplay mechanics such as Jewel Thief, where players must collect assets to unlock devices.

#### Chest

The **Chest** is where you can store assets from the **Weapons** and **Items** categories to grant players through in-game chests or llamas.

To add assets to the **Chest** tab, select an asset from the **Weapons** or **Items** tab, then click **Add to Chest**.

### Details Panel

The right panel of your screen is the **Details** panel, which explains an asset's information and tags. Within this panel are two tabs that contain an asset's attributes and the tags associated with it.

#### Information Tab

Select the right tab on the **Details** panel (the information icon) to see a selected asset's details, such as rarity and damage statistics.

You can also select the information icon above the asset to view its details and tags.

#### Menu Tab

[
*](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/api/documentation/image/32b3703e-67f7-412b-b1d2-ad7f60685f60?resizing_type=fit)

**Tags** are ways to identify and categorize assets by theme and usage.

Within the **Details** panel, you can also select the menu icon to sort through assets in the Creative inventory by their tags. You can select one or multiple tags by selecting their checkboxes.

In this panel, you can further select tags through the **Union** and **Intersect** tabs. The **Union** tab shows every asset that falls in the selected tag. You can select the **Intersect** tab to only display assets that share similar tags.

Within the **Menu** tab, use the **Clear** and **Mode** functions to clear assets or switch between modes.

To clear the tags above the inventory assets, you can either press **ALT + C** on your keyboard or right-click them.

Press **ALT + F** on your keyboard to access **Mode**, which automatically switches between the **Union** and **Intersect** tabs.

### Profile

Click the character portrait at the top left of the sidebar to open your **Profile** options. Here you can choose your player tags, whether to display your online status, who can join your party, and your participation status. You can also change your matchmaking region, whether to display your language, and whether to display mutual friends.

### Social

This tab displays a list of all your friends and has the **Go Looking For Party** button to party up with a squad.

### Add Friends

This tab displays any friend invites you've received, a list of people you've played with recently, and a list of friend suggestions based on mutual friends.

### Looking for Party

Clicking the **Looking for Party** button opens another set of options for finding available players through tags similar to those you’ve used in your own profile and selecting **Shuffle** to find another group of players to play with.

### Voice Chat

In this tab, you can choose whether voice chat is on the **Game Channel** or the **Party Channel**. If you're chatting on the Game Channel, everyone in the game you're playing can hear you. If you chat on the Party channel, only people in your party will hear you. You can modify **Voice Options** at the bottom of the panel.

You can also modify Voice Chat options by clicking the **Open Voice Settings** button.

### Settings
**

*Click image to enlarge.*

Selecting the **Settings** option from the sidebar menu opens a new page. This is where you can find the **User Settings**. The User Settings allow you to change and customize a number of settings to tailor your game experience to your needs. The menu bar at the top of the settings page has 10 different settings.

-
**Video**: Determines your display and graphics settings.

-
**Volume**: Change the volume on different parts of the Fortnite experience. You can also set the sound quality, voice chat settings, and whether to play licensed audio or not.

-
**Game**: Set your language, movement, combat style, building, and tutorial tips to enhance your gaming experience. You can even determine your extra game options, replays, and energy saving settings.

-
**Game UI**: Determine what displays on your HUD and the reticle and damage feedback you receive.

-
Touch and Motion:** Change the gyro options to further customize your gaming experience.

-
**Mouse & Keyboard**: Set your mouse sensitivity and keyboard movement to determine how your player moves. Mouse Flight and Tank Settings determine how your character pilots airplanes and tanks in-game.

-
**Keyboard Controls**: Create presets for keybinding to the mouse or keyboard. Change which keys control the character movement in-game.

-
**Controller**: Determine how your character moves, draws a weapon, as well as the controller deadzone, and sensitivity of the controller.

-
**Controller Mapping**: Create controller binding presets based on different preset options. Set your controller platform and which buttons control the character's movement, building, and other character options.

-
**Account & Privacy**: Find your account information and social privacy settings as well as clipping, gameplay privacy, credits, and legal information.

## Hotkey and Keybinding Shortcuts in Fortnite Creative

**כותרת מקורית:** Hotkey and Keybinding Shortcuts in Fortnite Creative  
**כתובת מקור:** https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/hotkey-and-keybinding-shortcuts-in-fortnite-creative  
**מקור קלט:** `02_ממשק_משתמש_תפריטים_ומקשים(1).md`  
**היקף:** Creative / Shared בהתאם לעמוד המקור  
**מזהה עמוד:** `hotkey-and-keybinding-shortcuts-in-fortnite-creative`

A **hotkey** is a key or key combination that provides quick access to a specific function. These are also called **shortcut keys** or **keybindings**.

This page is about the hotkeys available when you're in [Create mode](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#create-mode) on a **Fortnite Creative** island.

The hotkeys for Create mode may be different from the hotkeys used when playing a Fortnite game.

Active hotkeys are shown on the left side of the screen. The **hotkey menu** changes based on what you're doing.

### Use Pickaxe Hotkeys

There are two basic sets of hotkeys when you have your **pickaxe** equipped.

You can toggle between the pickaxe and the **phone tool** by pressing the **` (backtick)** key above the Tab key on your keyboard.

#### Pickaxe (Default)

If you're holding your pickaxe, you will see:

#### Pickaxe While Flying

If you double-tap the **spacebar**, the menu expands because you're now flying with your pickaxe.

##### Spacebar - Fly/Exit Fly (Double-Tap)

Double-tap the spacebar to toggle Fly **Off** or **On**. **Press and hold the spacebar** to fly up. Movement stops when you release the key.

This movement is different from when you use the **WASD keys** while flying.

##### L-Ctrl - Fly Down

Press and hold the **left Ctrl** key to fly down. This only works when flying is toggled **On**. (When you're not in Fly mode, pressing the Ctrl key will cause your avatar to crouch.)

##### C - Flight Speed

Use this key to toggle through a preset selection of flight speeds: **0.1x**, **0.5x** (slower than default), **1.0x** ( default speed), **1.5x**, **2.0x**, **3.0x**, **4.0x**, **5.0x**, **10.0x** (faster than default).

##### T - Heatmap

Use the **T** key to toggle the **heatmap**. You have three options: **Off**, **On**, and **Memory Values On**. For more on how the heatmap works, see the [Spatial Thermometer](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-the-spatial-thermometer-in-fortnite-creative#heatmap) page.

##### N - Phase (Off/On)

Phasing is when your avatar can pass through [objects](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#object) that it would normally [collide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary) with. Press the **N** key to toggle phasing **On** or **Off**.

##### M - Creative Menu

Press **M** to directly access the [Creative menu](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#creative-menu) tabs.

##### User Defined

If you set a custom keybind for Quick Menu, Island Settings, or Content Browser (as described in the **Set Up Custom Keybinds** section), this will display at the bottom of every hotkey menu. It will show the hotkey you chose and the name of the tab you associated with the hotkey. You can add multiple custom keybinds to your hotkey menus and they will all display.

When you're in Create mode, you'll use your phone tool to place objects and move them around. But if you want to test things like player mobility, equipping the pickaxe will give you functionality that's much closer to what your players get in-game. If you want a cleaner view of the island, the default pickaxe menu is much smaller than the other menus, giving you more view.

### Use Phone Tool Hotkeys

When you equip the phone tool, some of the hotkeys change.

Like the pickaxe hotkey menu, there are both default and flying menus for the phone tool. There are also other menus based on what you're doing with your phone tool.

#### Phone Tool (Default)

This is the menu that appears when you equip the phone tool.

There are hotkey options that the phone tool has that the pickaxe doesn't. These are covered below.

#### Phone Tool While Flying

When you have the phone tool equipped, you're creating your island. The new hotkeys are there to help you place and manipulate objects.

*When you're using the phone tool, and you double-tap the spacebar to fly, the menu changes.*

The phone tool hotkeys that the pickaxe doesn't have include the following.

##### Left mouse button - Copy

Point your mouse at a [prop](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#prop) or [device](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#device), then click your left mouse button to copy it. Use the mouse to move the copy where you want it, then click the left mouse button to place it. Press **Esc** or click the right mouse button to **unselect** the object. (There's more about the [Copy and Cut hotkeys](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/hotkey-and-keybinding-shortcuts-in-fortnite-creative) below.)

##### Right mouse button - Cut

Use this button to move an object. You are basically deleting the object then pasting it in a new location.

Point your mouse at the prop or device to select it, then click your right mouse button to cut it. Move it to a new location, click the left mouse button to place it, then press **Esc** to unselect it.

Pressing the right mouse button again **unselects** the object without placing it in a new location, and restores it to its previous location.

##### R - Select

When you select an object with the **R** key, it glows green.

To select multiple objects, hold the **R** key down and move the mouse to point at more objects and add them to the selection.

##### X - Delete

Point your phone at an object to select it, then press **X** to delete.

To delete multiple objects, hold the X key down and move the mouse pointer to each object you want to delete.

You can select up to 100 objects at a time. The menu will actually show how many objects you've selected.

##### M - Creative Menu

The **M** key opens the [Quick Menu](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/hotkey-and-keybinding-shortcuts-in-fortnite-creative), covered below. This hotkey option only shows when you're using the phone tool.

There are two more instances where the phone hotkey menu can change. These are when you [copy or cut an object](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/hotkey-and-keybinding-shortcuts-in-fortnite-creative), or when you want to [resize an object](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/hotkey-and-keybinding-shortcuts-in-fortnite-creative).

#### Copy, Cut and Place Objects

While using the **R** key to select an object makes it glow green, pointing your mouse at a prop or device to select it makes it glow blue.

When an object is blue, you can either **copy** the object (left mouse button), or **cut** it (right mouse button).

When you copy or cut an object, the blue glow gets darker, and the hotkey menu changes:

##### Left mouse button - Paste

The left mouse button now becomes the **Paste** button.

##### F - Push

Press and hold the **F** key to push a selected object away from you.

##### C - Pull

Press and hold the **C** key to pull it toward you.

##### R - Rotate Clockwise

Rotate the object clockwise (to the right) on the selected [axis](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#axis) (see **Tab Rotation Axis** below).

##### E - Rotate Counterclockwise

Rotate the object counterclockwise (to the left) on the selected axis.

##### Tab - Rotation Axis (Pitch, Yaw, Roll)

Any 3D object has three [axes](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#axis), pitch, yaw, and roll. Use the Tab key to toggle through these options. You can rotate the object along any axis, but can only select one axis at a time.

##### Tab - Rotation Reset (Hold)

Press and hold the **Tab** key to return the object to its original position with no axis rotation.

##### G - Drops (On, Off)

When Drops is **Off**, you can place an object in complete defiance of gravity.

When you toggle Drops to **On**, a line extends down to show where it will land when you paste (place) it.

##### V - Grid Snap (16)

An island is divided into [grids](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#grid). With grid snap, you can control how precisely an object snaps to a position, or aligns, with a grid when you place it. Grid snap becomes important when you're building walls or fences or anything else that requires careful alignment.

When set to **1**, the snap aligns things to the edge of a grid tile. Setting the grid snap higher makes it snap in place in smaller increments as each increment divides the grid into smaller units. You can set it up to 32, which means you can place with a precision as fine as one thirty-second of the full grid size.

You can also turn grid snap off, which lets you place an object without restraint.

Press the **V** key to toggle through the following grid increments: **32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1, Off**.

##### V - Collision (Hold) [Nothing]

This menu option can only be changed from the [Quick Menu](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/hotkey-and-keybinding-shortcuts-in-fortnite-creative) covered below, and is here just for informational purposes.

##### Left mouse button - Exit

Use the left mouse button to unselect an object.

##### Resize - (Hold) [Off/On]

Press and hold the left mouse button to toggle the **Resize** option **On** and **Off**. When set to On, the hotkey menu changes again. See **Resize an Object** below.

#### Resize an Object

To resize an object, you first need to select it for copy or cut with your left or right mouse button, then click and hold the right mouse button to toggle Resize to **On**.

When you toggle Resize to **On**, four new hotkeys will show up in the hotkey menu.

##### R - Grow

Pressing and holding the **R** key will increase the size until you release the key.

##### E - Shrink

Pressing and holding **E** will shrink it.

##### Tab - Resize Axis [All, Width, Depth, Height]

When you resize an object, you can resize it uniformly, or you can select a single **axis** (width, depth or height) to resize in just one direction.

##### Tab Reset (Hold)

Press and hold the **Tab** key to return the object to its original size.

#### Devices and Hotkeys

Like with props, you can copy, cut, move and resize devices, but there are a couple of hotkeys that work a little differently with devices than they do with props.

Like with props, when you point your phone at a device it will glow blue. But unlike a prop, if you step close enough to the device, you get an option to customize the device.

Press the **E** key to open the Customize panel.

Customization details are covered in the page for each device.

There's another thing that makes devices stand out from other objects when it comes to hotkeys.

Some devices, like the pickup truck below, have direct player interactions. In this example, highlighting the truck itself provides a hotkey option to drive the truck.

To customize the Pickup Truck Spawner device, you have to point to the device base, not the truck.

Some devices with player interactions don't have a base. This means that sometimes, when you point to the device and get close enough, you'll get both **interaction** and **customization** hotkeys showing together.

### Quick Menu

The Quick Menu option only shows up on phone tool hotkey menus.

Press the **M** key to access the Creative menu tabs, then select **Quick Menu**.

You can also set up a custom keybind to access the Quick Menu if this is something you use frequently. For how to do this, see [Set Up Custom Keybinds](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/setupcustomkeybinds) below.

The Quick Menu options are sorted into three groups.

#### Movement

##### Invulnerable - (Everything, Nothing)

When set to everything, you are safe from damage by anything while in Create mode.

-

-
Faster than default speed: **1.5x, 2.0x, 3.0x, 4.0x, 5.0x, 10.0x**

This setting can be changed from here or from the hotkey menu. Any changes you make here are mirrored on the hotkey menu.

##### Editor Camera - (External, Immersive)

With an external camera, you can see your avatar moving around the island. With immersive, you're seeing the island from the viewpoint of your avatar. This applies only to your view while in Create mode, and does not affect gameplay.

#### Object Placement

##### Drops - (Off, On)

Controls whether objects drop to the ground when placed. Leave this Off if you want to suspend objects in the air.

This can be changed here or from the hotkey menu. Changes here are mirrored on the hotkey menu.

##### Collision - (Everything, Nothing, Terrain)

When placing and moving objects, you can control what gets in the way:

-
**Everything** (the default) means that collision is turned on for terrain and other objects.

-
**Terrain** will restrict collision to the ground only.

-
**Nothing** turns off all collisions, which means you can partially bury objects or even place them all the way underground!

This setting can only be changed here, but the value you set will show on the hotkey menu.

##### Collide with Copies - (On, Off)

Controls whether objects will collide with recently placed objects when you move them around, or whether they'll phase through.

This can be changed here or from the hotkey menu. Changes here are mirrored on the hotkey menu.

##### Grid Snap - (Off, On)

This lets you control the precision of your placement. When set to **1**, the snap aligns things to the edge of a grid [tile](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/fortnite-glossary#tile). Setting the grid snap higher makes it snap in place in smaller increments. You can set it up to **32**, which means you can place with a precision as fine as one thirty-second of the full grid size.

This can be changed here or from the hotkey menu. Changes here are mirrored on the hotkey menu.

##### Building as Prop - (Disabled, Origin Edge, Origin Center)

Props can generally have any **transform** (position, rotation, and scale). Fortnite Building pieces are special, and their transforms must align to the Fortnite Building grid. This option lets you take a Fortnite Building piece and place it as though it were a prop, but it loses the ability to support other building pieces.

##### Hold Position - (From Left, Centered, From Right, As Selected)

This determines where the phone will grab and hold an object when you are placing it. This can be useful, for example, if you're building something, such as a fence or a wall, where you want to precisely align the objects.

##### Pull to Player - (Off, On)

#### Tools

##### Phone Tool Keybind - (Fast, Standard)

If you're using a controller instead of a mouse, you can use this to set whether you access the Quick Bar with a tap or a hold of the pickaxe button.

### Set Up Custom Keybinds

Select the **Keyboard Controls** tab, then scroll down to **Creative**.

-
**Column 1:** Assign any key from your keyboard. This also adds the key and description to your keyboard hotkey menu.

-
**Column 2:** Assign a second key from your keyboard. This gives you the option of setting up two buttons to do the same action.

Use the **red X** in a row to clear any binds you have set.

Through Fortnite, you can access **Battle Royale**, **Zero Build - Battle Royale**, and more games and experiences made by **Epic Games**. Many more are made by island creators and developers who don't work for Epic but take advantage of the free **Fortnite Creative** toolkit to build their own games.

And if you don't find the game you want to play, you too can build your own!

You can play Fortnite games on PC, Mac, and a range of consoles and mobile devices.

### Chapters and Seasons

Fortnite Battle Royale releases come in chapters, and each chapter is made up of seasons. A new season drops every two or three months, and brings with it a bunch of new outfits, vehicles, weapons, and other gameplay features to keep players, old and new, on their toes.

Each new season also comes with a video that introduces some of the cool new things you can expect.

Like the Fortnite seasons, the user interface (UI) also changes frequently, so don't be surprised if the images on this page don't match what you see on your screen!

### The Lobby

This is the Fortnite lobby, where the fun begins.

From here, you can find friends, select from different products and game modes, or go to Fortnite Creative to make your own island.

#### The Nav Bar

-
**PLAY:** Click Play to return to the lobby.

-
**LOCKER:** Where you keep your outfits and other cosmetics — anything that affects the appearance of your avatar. This is also where you go to change your outfit.

-
**SHOP:** Feeling like having a makeover? Go shopping in the Item Shop! But make sure you bring your V-Bucks (an in-game currency you can use to purchase Battle Passes, cosmetics, and other cool stuff). And in case you were wondering, new items are offered with every new season.

-
**BATTLEPASS:** The **Battle Pass** is a featured item in Battle Royale that players can use to unlock in-game cosmetics, V-Bucks, and other fun stuff. When you buy a Battle Pass for the current season, it also unlocks additional quests and challenges.

-
**QUESTS:** A quest is a task or objective you can complete to gain a reward. The most common reward is experience points, or XP, that you can use to unlock more features in a game.

-
**COMPETE:** Find out what competitions are happening, when, and their details. TL:DR: Fortnite Champion Series, or FNCS, is a high-profile Fortnite esports competition.

-
**CAREER:** Want to know how your game scores compare to your friends' scores? Click Profile. Want to study a game you played, or a game someone else shared a replay of? Click Replays.

-
**V-BUCKS:**You can purchase V-Bucks here!

#### Select a Game

The lobby is where you select a game then press **PLAY!** to start that game.

Some games provide options for the number of players, ranging from solo to squads (usually teams of four).

If you have this option, you can change your team size before you click **Play**.

If you don't have enough friends to fill your team, random teammates will be added, so don't worry if you have an odd number of friends who want to play, or if you want to play on a squad but are playing by yourself.

If you don’t want to play a game from Creative with random people, select the **Private Game** option above the play button.

To start a game quickly or enter ranked competition, select the **No Fill** and **Ranked** options when selecting a **mode**. No Fill assigns bots to the lobby so you don’t have to wait for more people to join the game.

#### Select a Different Game

But what if you want to play a different game?

You can either click the **search icon** to go to the **Search** page or scroll down to see what’s in Discover. To learn more, see [Exploring Discover](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-discover-in-fortnite-creative).

#### Play with Friends

Party up with your friends by clicking your player icon in the top right-hand corner.

This opens a whole set of new options.

For more about what you can do from here, see [Exploring the Sidebar and Game Menu](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/exploring-the-sidebar-and-game-menu-in-fortnite-creative).

Take a second to click the **Emote **button (because you can, and because it's fun!) then click **Play**!
